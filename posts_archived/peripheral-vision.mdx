I'm sitting in the back of class, right near the door, when I see it. 
Right in the corner of my eye.
I can never quite make out what it is, but it always sits in the corner of my eye, even if I try to look at it.
Focus on your screen, and imagine there's something in the corner of the room or your vision lurking there standing just beyond it.
That's the way it appears.

It comes every day at what are seemingly random times and just stands there.
From what I can tell, it's just observing me, maybe even taunting me since I can never truly see it.
Despite its daily routine, I can never get used to its deeply unsettling presence. It's suffocating.

Today was different, though. 
Usually when I try to look at it, as any curious person would, it shifts to stay out of my line of sight.
This time, I looked right at it and it stood there, staring back at me. I felt all the blood drain from my face.
Just a couple dozen feet in front of me stood this grotesque amalgamation of matter.
Even fully in focus, I still couldn't quite make out what or rather, who, it was.
It had a fragmented appearance, never converging to a specific form but it looked vaguely familiar, like a face I couldn't quite pick out.
It had these claw-like hands that were reaching out, not to me, but to something unseen, never seeming to be able to reach it.
It had no eyes, rather two hollow sockets that seemed to suck all the light in. That's what I remember the most. Those eyes. 

When I made eye contact, if you can call it that, with it, everything got dark.
Not pitch black and not like the lights turned off, but as if a violent thunderstorm had suddenly formed and blocked out the sun.
It started to walk towards me, jerking and stuttering around like a broken film reel.
Terrified, I got out of my chair and started backing towards the door.
It kept following me, and started to increase its pace.
I quickened mine as well, until it started to morph into some quadrupedal abomination, increasing its pace from a brisk jog to a motivated run.
I started sprinting down the hall, unable to scream. Why couldn't I breathe? 

I didn't have time to worry about that right now, as I could feel it catching up with me.
I try to increase my pace and I can sense it on my heels and the ceiling is falling and the walls are closing in.
I can hear it now, a familiar laughter, small phrases with an accent I've heard before, wait, why was it laughing? and then I feel its disgusting claw graze my ankle.
I catch of a glimpse of its red and white ombré nails digging into my skin, I trip and fall to the ground and turn around expecting to meet this creature face-to-face except there's nothing there.
There was never anything. I never even left the classroom. I look out the window, and it's a cloudless, sunny day.
A plane flies by, and the palm trees are rocking gently in the wind. 

What is happening to me?
